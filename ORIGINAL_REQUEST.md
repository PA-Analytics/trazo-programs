Integrate the physical 2.5D TRAZO Implementation Companion (mascot) into the React Flow quest map, supporting its 5 core visual states (IDLE, ATTENTION, THINKING, MOVING, VERIFIED/TRAZO MODE), an anchored conversation popover, real-geometry edge travel, and micro-reactions without degrading map performance or violating the product visual hierarchy.

## Context & Vision

The mascot physically inhabits the mission map as the living manifestation of TRAZO. It is not a decorative chatbot widget nor an intrusive NPC. It lives beside the active mission node, enters an ATTENTION state when a decision or clarification is needed, walks along real SVG edge paths with body orientation and bobbing, anchors a compact conversation panel on click, and triggers "Modo TRAZO" upon genuine verified actions (PASS).

---

## Requirements

### R1. Companion Layer & 2.5D Physical Inhabitation
- Mount the mascot inside the React Flow viewport layer so it naturally scales and pans with hardware acceleration.
- Implement directional body orientation across 8 compass angles (`N`, `NE`, `E`, `SE`, `S`, `SW`, `W`, `NW`) derived dynamically from movement vectors or map focal points.
- Include a dynamic ground drop shadow with ambient occlusion (scaling/fading with bounce height) and dynamic Y-sorting Z-index.

### R2. 5 Core Visual States & Micro-Interactions
- Support exactly 5 core visual states: `IDLE`, `ATTENTION`, `THINKING`, `MOVING`, and `VERIFIED` (Modo TRAZO).
- **IDLE**: Subtle breathing/glance near the active mission, zero visual clutter.
- **ATTENTION**: Small antenna shift and cobalt cue when TRAZO has a recommendation or clarification.
- **THINKING**: Discrete trail/antenna tracking during async AI inference (no generic spinners).
- **MOVING**: Real-time traversal along the actual SVG edge geometry at constant arc speed.
- **VERIFIED (Modo TRAZO)**: Cobalt waypoint activation, trail illumination, and restrained satisfaction ("yep. eso sí") when an action passes verification.
- **Micro-reactions**: Hover eye tracking, quick repeated tap reaction, long idle repositioning.

### R3. Anchored Compact Companion Panel
- Clicking/tapping the mascot toggles an anchored, compact conversation panel connected to the real TRAZO next-action & evidence evaluation engine.
- When closed, only the physical mascot remains on the map canvas.
- No permanent large overlay obscuring the graph.

### R4. Performance & Motion Accessibility
- Decouple the 60/120 FPS animation loop from React's state tree (direct GPU `transform: translate3d` via refs).
- Full compliance with `prefers-reduced-motion`: instant teleportation with subtle fade, eliminating continuous motion.
- `aria-hidden="true"` on the decorative sprite and accessible announcements (`aria-live="polite"`) on significant state transitions.
